# IPA Information
![[../assets/ipa.jpg]]
India Pale Ales (IPAs) are typically fairly strong with regards to alcohol content (7 - 9% by volume) and are characteristically hoppy.  The hops used typically provide bitterness, but also different flavors that are floral or citrusy. The grain used is typically a Pale malt, as the name implies. 
## History
IPAs were created by the British when they were making long trips to India.  Hops are a preservative, so they added extra hops to the beer to help keep it fresh for the journey.  Today it's a very common craft and homebrew style.
## Commercial Examples
- Hopadillo
- Stash
- Stone
- Torpedo
