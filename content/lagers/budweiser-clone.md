## Description
Budweiser is an American Lager with about 5% alcohol by volume with a clean crisp taste.  Not much bitterness or other hoppy flavors.  It is very well established in the U.S. and worldwide.  This recipe is a clone so it may not match exactly what the brewery does, but it will produce similar results.
