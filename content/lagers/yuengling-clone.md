## Description
Yuengling Lager, brewed in Pennsylvania, is one of the oldest Lagers in the country, but doesn't have a very large following.  The brewery does not do mass marketing like it's American counterparts and it's a traditional German Lager which not all Americans prefer.  That said, it has it's fans and is a successful high quality Lager with a balance of malt and hop flavor.
