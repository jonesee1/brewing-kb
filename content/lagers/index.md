# Lager Information
Lagers are similar to ales except for the fermentation process.  Ales are fermented at room temperature while Lagers are cold fermented.  The yeast used is bred to operate in colder environments.  This gives Lagers a less complex but often refreshing flavor profile.  This is particularly noticeable in American Lagers like the Miller, Budweiser and Coors brands.
## History
It's generally believed that Lagers originated hundreds of years ago in Germany or adjacent areas in central Europe.  A local strain of yeast was found to tolerate colder temperatures so they started fermenting in colder temperatures.  With that discovery a new style was born and eventually made it's way to the U.S. via Anheuser-Busch, Miller and Coors all founded in the 1800s.
