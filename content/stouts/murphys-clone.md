## Description
Murphy's Irish Stout is similar to Guinness with it's coffee like flavor, but has a bit more sweetness to it.  The brewery was founded after Guinness so it is likely an attempt to make a similar beer to break into the market and it did.  It's a solid Stout and worth a taste if you like this style.
