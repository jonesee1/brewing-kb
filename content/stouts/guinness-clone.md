## Description
Guinness is the icon for stouts.  Brewed in Dublin Ireland since the 1700's, it is still the Stout to have as long as you find a pub or bar that can make a proper pour.  A proper pour of Guinness involves two steps.  First you hold a pint glass at 45 degree angle and pull the tap handle down to fill it about 3/4 full.  You then let it rest for about 1 -2 minutes.  When settled, you top of the pint by pushing the tap handle forward and this creates the creamy foam or head Guinness is known for.  The final note regarding Guinness is it's surprisingly low in alcohol.  Typically around 4% by volume.
