# Stout/Porter Information
Stouts are largely defined by one brand commercially, Guinness.  There are many others but none are as iconic as the one from St. James's Gate brewery in Dublin Ireland.  Stouts are dark ales brewed with a chocolate malt giving them a coffee like flavor.  While there are exceptions stouts are typically not very strong with regards to alcohol and don't have a lot of flavor from hops.  This category also includes Porters which are almost identical to stouts with very little to distinguish one from the other.
## History
Stouts were born in the 1700s out of the porter style in England.  Both are produced by using dark grains and typically contain very minimal hop flavor.  The style was popular in England due to it's rich flavor and heavy mouth feel.  It made it's way to Ireland leading to Guinness and to the U.S. where craft brewers have taken the baton and created or recreated new versions of porters and stouts.
