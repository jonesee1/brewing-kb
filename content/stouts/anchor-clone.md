## Description
Anchor brewing company, founded in California, produces a Porter/Stout simply named Anchor Porter.  It is dark and malty with chocolate, coffee and a touch of sweetness without much hop influence.  It's not a widespread, mainstream beer, but it has it's fans amongst craft and homebrewers.
