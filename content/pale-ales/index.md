# Pale Ale Information
Pale Ales represent a wide variety of Ales.  IPAs and Pale Ales are nearly identical with the exception of the amount hops used and alcohol by volume.  Pale Ales have significant grain and hop flavor, but generally not as much an IPA.   
## History
Pale Ales originated in England as an alternative to [[stouts/index |Stouts]].  Lighter grains provided a different flavor profile compared to Stouts of the day.  The style became very popular in England and eventually made it's way to the U.S., mostly through craft brewers.  Several of those craft brewers would grow to become full nationwide commercial breweries like Sierra Nevada.  
## Commercial Examples
- Bass Pale Ale
- Dales Pale Ale
- Sierra Nevada Pale Ale
