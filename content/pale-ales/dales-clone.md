## Description
Dale's Pale Ale is brewed by Oskar Blues brewery, founded in Colorado.  It is a traditional American Pale Ale with significant flavor from the hops, but well balanced with some malty flavors.  While not as widely popular as some other American beers, it has a strong following in the U.S. and is worth a try.
