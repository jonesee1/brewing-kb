## Description
Sierra Nevada Pale Ale (SNPA) is one of the most popular Pale Ales in the U.S.. It's hop signature jumps out and is consistent with all of the beers Sierra Nevada produces.  The dominant flavor comes from Cascade hops added late in the boil and that's signature of the Sierra Nevada brewery.
